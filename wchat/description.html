How It Works

Simple by design.

Getting started with WChat takes less than a minute. The entire experience is engineered to get out of your way so you can focus on the conversation.

01
Create Your Account

Sign up with just an email and a username. No phone number required. No personal data harvested. Your account belongs to you alone.

02
Find Someone

Search for any WChat user by their unique username. Add them as a contact or message directly — no waiting for approval or friend requests.

03
Open a Private Chat

Every new conversation instantly creates an encrypted private channel. No one else can see or join this conversation — it exists only between you two.

04
Communicate Freely

Type, send files, share voice notes, and express yourself without restriction. Your history is synced securely and accessible only on your devices.

Privacy Architecture

Built private
from the ground up.

Privacy isn't a checkbox on WChat — it's the architecture. Every system decision, from how data is stored to how keys are managed, was made with your privacy as the first constraint, not an afterthought.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

WChat's servers store only encrypted ciphertext. Keys are generated and held exclusively on user devices — the server cannot decrypt any message.

No Metadata Logging

We do not log who you message, how often, or when. Conversation metadata is stripped and never retained beyond the moment of delivery.

Open Protocol

WChat's encryption protocol is open-source and independently audited. Anyone can inspect exactly how your privacy is protected.

No Ads, No Data Sales

WChat is funded by users, not advertisers. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data in any form. The business model is the product — not you.
